## STAGE 4: SCORING SYSTEM (Automated Calculation)

```
PROBABILITY_SCORE (0-10):
= (Historical_Win_Rate + Regime_Alignment_Bonus) × Confluence_Factor
Example: (72% + 10% bonus) × 1.15 = 94.3% → Score 9.4/10

EXPECTED_RETURN_SCORE (0-10):
= (Target - Entry) ÷ Entry × 100 (normalized to 0-10 scale)
Example: (₹1,568 - ₹1,545) ÷ ₹1,545 × 100 = 1.49% → Score 7.5/10

RISK_SCORE (0-10, where lower = better):
= (Entry - Stop) ÷ Entry × 100 (normalized inverse)
Example: (₹1,545 - ₹1,538) ÷ ₹1,545 × 100 = 0.45% → Score 2.8/10

QUALITY_METRIC (Final Score):
= (Probability_Score × Expected_Return_Score) ÷ Risk_Score
= (9.4 × 7.5) ÷ 2.8 = 25.1 → ULTRA ELITE (>10.0 range)

RECOMMENDATION MAPPING:
├─ Quality ≥10.0 → ELITE CONVICTION (Position 1.5-2% risk, pyramid entry)
├─ Quality 8.0-9.9 → EXCELLENT (Position 1% risk, standard entry)
├─ Quality 6.5-7.9 → GOOD (Position 0.75% risk, tight SL)
├─ Quality <6.5 → SKIP (Watchlist only, "No edge detected")
```
